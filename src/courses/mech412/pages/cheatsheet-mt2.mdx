## 1 · Electric circuits (L9)

- R: $e=iR$, $Z=R$. C: $i=C\dot e$, $e=\tfrac1C\!\int\!i$, $Z=\tfrac1{Cs}$. L: $e=L\dot i$, $Z=Ls$. Energy $\tfrac12Li^2$, $\tfrac12Ce^2$.
- **Transformer** ratio $1:m$: $e_{34}=m\,e_{12}$, $i_b=i_a/m$ (impedance seen $\times m^2$). **Gyrator**: $e_2=ri_1$, $e_1=-ri_2$.
- KVL (sign rule: $\ominus\to\oplus$ is $-V$) and KCL at nodes. **Divider** $\dfrac{E_{out}}{E_{in}}=\dfrac{Z_1}{Z_1+Z_2}$ ($Z_1$ across the output).
- **Double divider** ($Z_1$ series, $Z_2$ shunt, $Z_3$ series, $Z_4$ shunt): $\dfrac{E_o}{E_i}=\dfrac{Z_2Z_4}{Z_1(Z_2+Z_3+Z_4)+Z_2(Z_3+Z_4)}$.
- Twin-T notch: $\dfrac{R^2C^2s^2+1}{R^2C^2s^2+4RCs+1}$. Bridged-T ($R_1=0.5,R_2=1,C=0.5$): $\dfrac{s^2+4s+8}{s^2+8s+8}$ (dip $-6$ dB at $\sqrt8$).

## 2 · Op-amps (L10)

$V^-=V^+$, no input current. **Always take $i$ at nodes.** Inverting: $V_o=-\dfrac{Z_2}{Z_1}V_i$ ($Z_{||}=\dfrac R{RCs+1}$, $Z_{series}=R+\tfrac1{Cs}$). PID: $-\dfrac{(R_1C_1s+1)(R_2C_2s+1)}{R_1C_2s}$, $K_p=-\big(\tfrac{C_1}{C_2}+\tfrac{R_2}{R_1}\big)$, $K_i=-\tfrac1{R_1C_2}$, $K_d=-R_2C_1$. Finite gain $K$: $\dfrac{E_o}{E_i}=\dfrac{K\,H_{in}}{1+\beta K}\to\dfrac{H_{in}}\beta$. Buffer: transfer function unchanged by the load. MFB band-pass: $-\dfrac{(C_1R_3/R_1)s}{C_1C_2R_3s^2+(C_1+C_2)s+\frac1{R_1}+\frac1{R_2}}$. Current amplifier $I_a/V_{in}=-R/(R_{in}R_s)$.

## 3 · Linearisation (L10, tutorials 3–6)

$F(x,u)\approx F(\bar x,\bar u)+A(x-\bar x)+B(u-\bar u)$, $A=\partial F/\partial x$, $B=\partial F/\partial u$. **Circuit recipe:** states $e_C,i_L$; KCL/KVL; split $e_i=\bar e_i+\hat e_i$ etc.; **derivatives zero for $\bar e,\bar\imath$**; Taylor-expand only the nonlinear element; incremental resistance $=1/(di/de)$ (e.g. $\tfrac38\bar e^2$ for $e^3/8$; $2|\bar e|$ for $|e|e$); constant sources vanish. **Never use Laplace or impedances on the nonlinear equation.** Maglev (gap up): $\dfrac{\Delta Z}{\Delta I}=\dfrac{2g/i_0}{s^2+2g/z_0}$.

## 4 · Analogies (L11, handout 2)

| mechanical | mobility ($u$ potential, $f$ flux) | impedance ($f$ potential, $u$ flux) |
|---|---|---|
| mass $M_M$ | capacitor $M_M$ (to ground) | inductor $M_M$ |
| spring $k$ | inductor $C_M=1/k$ | capacitor $C_M=1/k$ |
| dashpot $R_M$ | resistor $1/R_M$ | resistor $R_M$ |
| velocity source | voltage source | current source |
| force source | current source | voltage source |

**Rules:** force source first; nodes = absolute velocities; wall elements to ground, mass-to-mass elements between nodes; impedance = dual; **lever or pivot = transformer** (mobility $(m_2/m_1):1$, impedance $(m_1/m_2):1$; arm ratio, impedance $\propto$ ratio$^2$); pivoted bar: $I_O=I_G+md^2$. Mechanical *impedance* $\to$ acoustic: transformer $S:1$; mechanical *mobility* $\to$ acoustic *impedance*: gyrator $1/S$.

## 5 · DC motors and transducers (L12)

$\tau=K_tI_fi_a$, $e_b=K_b\omega$; $e_i=R_ai_a+L_a\dot i_a+e_b$; $J\dot\omega+B\omega=\tau-\tau_L$.
$$\frac{\Omega}{E_i}=\frac{K_t}{(L_as+R_a)(Js+B)+K_bK_t}\quad(\textbf{plus sign}),\qquad\frac\Theta{E_a}=\frac{K}{s[L_aJs^2+(L_aB+R_aJ)s+R_aB+KK_b]} .$$
Voice coil: $\dfrac{U_x}{V}=\dfrac{Bl}{(Ls+R)(ms+b+k/s)+(Bl)^2}$. Armature loop with a sensing resistor: $V_A-V_B=I_AR_A+K_e\omega$, $V_B=I_AR_S$. Gear train referred to shaft 1: $J_{eq}=J_1+(n_1/n_2)^2J_2$.

## 6 · Acoustic (L13)

Pressure = potential, volume flow $U$ = flux. $R_A=d\Delta p/dU$, $M_A=\rho L/A$ (neck, **series L**), $C_A=V/(\rho c_0^2)$ (cavity, **shunt C**). Mass-flux resistances: $R_A=\rho R_f$, $M_A=\rho M_f$, $C_A=C_f/\rho$. Open side tube = shunt $M_A$; horn = series $M_A$ ending in $R_A$.

## 7 · Pneumatic (L14)

Nozzle–flapper gap $x=\dfrac b{a+b}e-\dfrac a{a+b}y$, $P_b=K_1x$, $P_c=Kx$. RC cavity with piston: $\dfrac{X}{P_i}=\dfrac{A/k}{R(C+A^2/k)s+1}$ (**$C+A^2/k$**, not $C+1/(kA^2)$). Pneumatic PID (high gain $K$): feedback $\dfrac1{R_dCs+1}-\dfrac1{R_iCs+1}$, $\dfrac{P_c}{E}\to\dfrac{bk}{aA}\dfrac{(R_dCs+1)(R_iCs+1)}{(R_i-R_d)Cs}=K_p(1+\tfrac1{T_is}+T_ds)$, $T_i\approx R_iC$, $T_d\approx R_dC$. Two-bellows PID (T8 Q2): $K_p=\tfrac{bk}{aA}(1+\tfrac{R_1}{R_2}+\tfrac{R_1C_1}{R_2C_2})$, $K_d\propto R_1C_1$, $K_i\propto\tfrac1{R_2C_2}$. PD is $K_p(1+T_ds)$ (the form $K_p(1+1/(T_ds))$ is PI).

## 8 · Hydraulic and liquid level (L15)

- Tank: inflow $-$ outflow $=C\dot h$ ($C$ = area, or $A/\rho g$ for pressure); valve $q=h/R$ or $(h_1-h_2)/R$; linearised orifice $R=2H/Q$. Two tanks superposition (T8 Q1): $\dfrac{H_2}{Q_i}=\dfrac{R_2}{(R_1C_1s+1)(R_2C_2s+1)}$ (tank 2 loaded only through tank 1), $\dfrac{H_2}{Q_d}=\dfrac{R_2}{R_2C_2s+1}$; **interacting:** $\dfrac{Q_{out}}{Q_{in}}=\dfrac1{R_1R_2C_1C_2s^2+(R_1C_1+R_2C_2+R_2C_1)s+1}$.
- **Dashpot** (massless cylinder): $\dfrac ZY=\dfrac{Ts}{Ts+1}$ (cylinder free, washout) or $\dfrac1{Ts+1}$ (cylinder fixed, lag), $T=\dfrac{\rho RA^2}k$ (mass flux) or $\dfrac{RA^2}k$ (volume flux); seen through the transformer $R_M=\rho R_fA_p^2$.
- **Servo** $\dfrac YX=\dfrac Ks$ (integrator); lever $x=\dfrac b{a+b}e-\dfrac a{a+b}z$; closed loop $\dfrac{bK(Ts+1)}{(a+b)s(Ts+1)+aK}\to\dfrac ba(Ts+1)$ (lag feedback gives PD). Governor with washout feedback $\dfrac{bs}{bs+k}$: $K_p=\dfrac{a_2}{a_1}$, $T_i=\dfrac bk$ (PI).
- Bellows + hydraulic servo (T8 Q3): $\dfrac Y{P_i}=\dfrac{A/k}{s/K_H+1}$.
- Thermal: $R_t=1/(hA)$, $C_t=mc_p$.

## 9 · Sensitivity and errors (L16–L17 preview)

$S^T_K=\dfrac{\partial T/T}{\partial K/K}$; **chain rule** $S^T_K=S^T_GS^G_K$; unity feedback $S^T_G=\dfrac1{1+L}$, $S^T_H=-\dfrac{GH}{1+GH}$. Open-loop error $(1-G)R$, closed-loop $\dfrac R{1+G_cG}$. Final-value theorem **only for a stable loop**. A disturbance entering after the controller: offset $-A/(\text{loop DC gain})$.

<Callout kind="warn" title="Slips to avoid">

Back emf: **plus** $K_bK_t$. Bellows: $C+A^2/k$. PD vs PI labels. $e_{34}=m\,e_{12}$. Dashpot: which end is fixed. Incremental resistance is the **slope**, not $\bar e/\bar\imath$. Impedance capacitor of a spring is its compliance $1/k$.

</Callout>
