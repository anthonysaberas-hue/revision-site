<Problem id="P1" title="Problem 1 (25, mandatory): turbojet at 10 000 m, all calculations on stagnation conditions" tags="turbojet, ram, stagnation, nozzle, thrust">

<Statement>

A turbojet (diffuser, compressor, burner, turbine, convergent-divergent nozzle, stations 1 to 6) flies at 10 000 m on a standard day (use the standard-atmosphere table: $T_1=223.26$ K, $P_1=26.5$ kPa) at 270 m/s with an air flow of 0.50 kg/s. The compressor stagnation pressure ratio is 15; efficiencies: diffuser 100 %, compressor 85 %, turbine 90 %, nozzle 100 %; maximum stagnation temperature 1400 K; for air $c_{p}=1.005$ kJ/kg·K, $k=1.4$; for the gas $c_p=1.147$ kJ/kg·K, $k=1.333$. All calculations are on stagnation conditions, the stagnation pressure is constant through the combustor, the fuel/air ratio is 0.020636, and the nozzle is convergent-divergent. Fill a table: (a) inlet Mach number, (b) the ram pressure rise or pressure ratio in bars, (c) compressor work in kW, (d) turbine work in kW, (e) turbine exit pressure in bars, (f) nozzle exit pressure in bars, (g) nozzle exit velocity in m/s, (h) thrust due to pressure and thrust due to momentum.

</Statement>

<Think>

A chain of stagnation states with one idea per station: **ram** (the flight speed raises $T_0$ and $P_0$ of the entering air), **compressor** (work from $\eta_C$), **turbine** (its work equals the compressor's, which fixes $T_{05}$; its efficiency then fixes $P_{05}$), **nozzle** (the C-D nozzle expands to ambient, so there is no pressure thrust). The fuel/air ratio is given, so the combustor needs no energy balance. Two points are ambiguous in the paper: what (b) wants, and whether the fuel mass counts in the exit momentum (below).

</Think>

<Plan>

$c$, $Ma$ → $T_{01}$, $P_{01}$ → $T_{03s}$, $T_{03}$, $\dot W_C$ → $T_{05}$, $T_{05s}$, $P_{05}$ → $T_6$, $V_6$ at $P_6=P_1$ → $F$.

</Plan>

<Steps>
<Step title="1. (a) Inlet Mach number">

$c_1=\sqrt{kRT_1}=\sqrt{1.4(287)(223.26)}=\mathbf{299.5}$ m/s (the text table gives the same). $Ma_1=\dfrac{270}{299.5}=\mathbf{0.9015}$. Copy ✔, notebook ✔ (0.9015).

</Step>
<Step title="2. (b) Ram: stagnation temperature and pressure at the diffuser exit">

$T_{01}=T_1+\dfrac{V^2}{2c_p}=223.26+\dfrac{270^2}{2000(1.005)}=259.53$ K. $\dfrac{P_{01}}{P_1}=\left(\dfrac{T_{01}}{T_1}\right)^{k/(k-1)}=\left(\dfrac{259.53}{223.26}\right)^{3.5}=\mathbf{1.694}$, so $P_{01}=1.694(26.5)=44.88$ kPa $=\mathbf{0.4488}$ bar; the **ram pressure rise** is $44.88-26.5=18.4$ kPa = 0.184 bar. </Step>
<Step title="3. (c) Compressor">

</Step>

</Step>

</Step>
