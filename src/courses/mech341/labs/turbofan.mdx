## Things to try

- Raise the **bypass ratio** at fixed core settings: thrust per unit of core flow rises and TSFC falls, because a large mass of air given a small velocity increase is a better trade for propulsive efficiency than a small mass given a large one.
- Raise the **turbine inlet T₀₄**: the combustor glows brighter and specific thrust grows, but more fuel is burned; compare the TSFC before and after.
- Raise the **flight Mach number**: ram compression adds to the pressure ratio, while the jet velocity barely changes, so propulsive efficiency climbs and net thrust drops (the intake momentum term grows).
- Increase the **altitude**: the colder ambient air lowers the compressor-inlet temperature, so the cycle runs at a higher temperature ratio, while thrust falls with the density.
