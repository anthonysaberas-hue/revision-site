## Things to try

- Switch on **reheat**: the turbine exit quality rises (less liquid in the last stages) and the efficiency edges up, because heat is added at a higher mean temperature.
- Add the **open feedwater heater**, then the **closed** one: each takes bleed steam, so the feedwater enters the boiler hotter and the efficiency rises, while the work per kg of boiler steam falls.
- Lower the **condenser pressure**: the cold reservoir temperature falls and the efficiency improves, but the turbine exit becomes wetter, which is the practical limit.
- Raise the **boiler pressure** with reheat off: efficiency rises, then the exit becomes wetter; this is why high pressures come with reheat.
- Lower the **turbine η_T** and watch the efficiency drop by several points: real components cost more than any cycle tweak.
