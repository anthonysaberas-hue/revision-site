## Things to try

- Drag the **outdoor temperature** down: the temperature lift grows, the COP falls and the electricity needed for the same heat rises, which is why a heat pump struggles on the coldest days.
- Compare with **resistance heating** (COP = 1): even at the coldest setting the heat pump still wins, because it moves heat instead of making it.
- Raise the **house heat-loss UA** (a leakier house): the required heat rate and the compressor power grow, but the COP does not change, because it depends only on the temperature levels.
