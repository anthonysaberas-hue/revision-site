# Rocket nozzle lab

<div className="lede">

A converging-diverging rocket nozzle: a chamber at 10 to 20 MPa and about 3500 K feeds a sonic throat, and the bell expands the gas to Mach 3 to 5 (for k = 1.2, A<sub>e</sub>/A* = 15 gives Ma<sub>e</sub> = 3.56). The throat sets the mass flow, the area ratio sets the exit Mach number, and the ambient pressure decides whether the plume is over- or under-expanded. A shock inside the nozzle belongs to MECH 430: this lab labels that regime and links to it.

## Things to try

- Slide the **altitude** upwards: the same nozzle goes from over-expanded (plume pinched by the ambient pressure) through its design point to under-expanded (plume flaring), and thrust rises because the (P<sub>e</sub> − P<sub>a</sub>) term grows.
- Increase the **area ratio**: the exit Mach number and exit velocity rise and the exit pressure drops; the thrust gain levels off while the bell gets longer and heavier.
- Raise the **chamber pressure** at fixed area ratio: mass flow and thrust scale with P₀ but the exit Mach number stays the same, because it depends only on the area ratio and k.
- Choose a high ambient pressure with a large area ratio: the regime label reports a shock inside the nozzle and links to the <a href="#/mech430/lab/engine">MECH 430 engine lab</a> instead of modelling it.
