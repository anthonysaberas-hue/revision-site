# Brayton cycle and turbojet

<div className="lede">

Use the **Brayton** mode for a gas-turbine problem with component efficiencies, a regenerator, and either constant $c_p$ and $k$ (the exam's cold-air-standard) or the variable-specific-heat air table A-17 ($h$ and $P_r$). Use the **Turbojet** mode for a flight problem: it builds the ambient state from the standard atmosphere A-16 (or from $T_a$, $P_a$), the ram rise, diffuser, compressor, burner, turbine and a converging or converging-diverging nozzle, and lists the stagnation temperature and pressure at stations 1 to 6 and the split of thrust into momentum and pressure terms. It replaces the air-table lookups and the long stagnation-state chain of the 2021 final, problem 1. Gas temperatures outside the 200 to 3000 K air table are flagged, never extrapolated.
