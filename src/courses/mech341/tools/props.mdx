# Fluid properties: water and R-134a

<div className="lede">

Use this when a problem hands you two properties of steam or R-134a and you need the rest: it replaces the superheated, saturated and compressed-liquid steam tables (A-4 to A-7) and the refrigerant tables (A-11 to A-13), including the interpolation you would do by hand. Pick the pair you know (P–T, P–x, T–x, P–h or P–s), read $v$, $u$, $h$, $s$, the phase and the quality, and see the state on a $T$–$s$ diagram. A state outside the tables (water $T>1300$ °C or $P>60$ MPa; R-134a $P>4$ MPa or $T$ outside $-40$ to $200$ °C) is flagged as "outside table range", never extrapolated.
