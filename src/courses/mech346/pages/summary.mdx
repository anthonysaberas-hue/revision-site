# MECH 346 on one page

<div className="lede">

Heat moves three ways. Every problem is: **find which ways are present, write each as a resistance or a flux, join them in series or parallel (or write an energy balance), and solve**. The rest of the course is a catalogue of how to get the coefficient $h$ and the factors that go with it.

</div>

## The big picture

| Mode | Law | Resistance | Driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conduction | $\dot Q=-kA\,dT/dx$ | $L/kA$, $\ln(r_2/r_1)/2\pi kL$, $(r_2-r_1)/4\pi kr_1r_2$ | $k$, geometry |
| Convection | $\dot Q=hA(T_s-T_\infty)$ | $1/hA$ | fluid, flow, geometry ($Nu$) |
| Radiation | $\dot Q=\varepsilon\sigma A(T_s^4-T_{surr}^4)$ | $\dfrac{1-\varepsilon}{\varepsilon A}$, $\dfrac1{AF}$ | $T^4$, $\varepsilon$, $F$ |

## Which chapter, which question

1. **Conduction (Ch 2–3, fins):** differential equation + boundary conditions; generation: $T_s$ by energy balance, centre rise $\dfrac{\dot er_0^2}{2nk}$ with $n=1,2,3$ for wall, cylinder, sphere; networks; critical radius $k/h$; fins $\sqrt{hpkA_c}\theta_b\tanh mL$.
2. **Transient (Ch 4):** compute $Bi$ → lumped $e^{-t/\tau}$ ($Bi\le0.1$) → one-term ($Fo>0.2$, Tables 4-2/4-3) → product for 2-D/3-D → semi-infinite (erfc, $\sqrt{\alpha t}$).
3. **Convection (Ch 6–9):** $h=Nu\,k/L$; find $Nu$ from the regime: plate $0.664Re^{1/2}Pr^{1/3}$ / $(0.037Re^{0.8}-871)Pr^{1/3}$; cylinder Churchill–Bernstein; tube 3.66 / Hausen / Gnielinski; natural $Ra$ correlations.
4. **Exit temperatures (Ch 7–8):** constant surface temperature $T_e=T_s-(T_s-T_i)e^{-hA/\dot mc_p}$; constant flux $T_e=T_i+q''A/\dot mc_p$.
5. **Heat exchangers (Ch 11):** $\dot Q=UA\Delta T_{lm}F$ or $\varepsilon C_{min}(T_{h,i}-T_{c,i})$.
6. **Radiation (Ch 12–13):** $\sigma T^4$, Wien, bands; view factors; surface resistances; shields; reradiating walls.

## Five reflexes

1. **Kelvin** for radiation and $\beta$.
2. **Regime before correlation:** $Re$, $Ra$, $Bi$, $Fo$.
3. **Properties at the right temperature:** film (external), bulk mean (internal), free stream (sphere).
4. **Energy balance at the surface** when only one temperature is known.
5. **Sanity check** the sign, the size and the limits ($0<\varepsilon<1$, $Q<Q_{max}$, $T_e$ between $T_i$ and $T_s$).
