## Table 4-2: one-term coefficients

$Bi=hL/k$ for the plane wall of thickness $2L$ (half-thickness $L$), $Bi=hr_0/k$ for the cylinder and the sphere. The characteristic equations: plane wall $\lambda_1\tan\lambda_1=Bi$; cylinder $\lambda_1J_1(\lambda_1)/J_0(\lambda_1)=Bi$; sphere $1-\lambda_1\cot\lambda_1=Bi$. The coefficients: $A_1^{wall}=\dfrac{4\sin\lambda_1}{2\lambda_1+\sin2\lambda_1}$, $A_1^{cyl}=\dfrac{2J_1(\lambda_1)}{\lambda_1[J_0^2(\lambda_1)+J_1^2(\lambda_1)]}$, $A_1^{sph}=\dfrac{4(\sin\lambda_1-\lambda_1\cos\lambda_1)}{2\lambda_1-\sin2\lambda_1}$.
