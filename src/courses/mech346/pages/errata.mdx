## Self-corrections made while building this site

- The first draft of the conduction-equation notes said the heat flux "drops at the interface" between a fuel rod and its steel tube. That is **wrong**: the flux is continuous at $r_1$ (same heat, same area) and falls gradually as $1/r$ through the tube. Fixed in <Ch slug="conduction-eq">Chapter 2</Ch> and the October 2021 paper.
- An early claim that the brass-cylinder answer was insensitive to $k$ was wrong (item 9); corrected after a numerical check.
- Wind-chill coefficients were removed from <Ch slug="intro">Chapter 1</Ch> because no source value could be verified.
